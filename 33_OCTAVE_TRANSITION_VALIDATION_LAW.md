## 4. Runes as state markers, not magic

Runes are not explanations of molecules, organs or physics.

They are **state markers** used by AI or humans to track transition risk.

```yaml
RUNE_STATE_MARKERS:
  "△": "pressure / tension / instability / reactive potential"
  "∅": "unknown mechanism / missing intermediate / not enough data"
  "∅✓": "unknown preserved; do not invent answer"
  "▣": "constraint / boundary / law / condition / safety"
  "⊙": "candidate transition / possible reaction / possible route"
  "⊙╳": "transition blocked / model unsupported / overclaim prevented"
  "◇✓": "stable within known conditions / validated enough for limited statement"
  "⚠": "risk: toxicity, explosion, instability, harm, misuse"
  "⟲△": "runaway cycle / feedback risk / autocatalytic drift"
```

Correct use:

```text
Rune marks a transition state.
Rune does not replace measurement.
```

```text

where new properties appear
```


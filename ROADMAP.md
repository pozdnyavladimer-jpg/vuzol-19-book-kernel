## Launch discipline

Do not launch first with:

```text
Earth as chip
Moon as clock
Nazca as firmware
Pyramid Tesla Crown
full cosmic model
```

Launch first with:

```text
AI answers too fast.
People act from shadow.
Vuzol-19 asks:
Does this intent have the right to become action?
```

## Main sentence

> **The roadmap is not to make the system louder.  
> It is to make the first door clear enough that someone can enter with one honest intent.**

# Vuzol-19 Community Code of Conduct

> **Vuzol-19 is a project about protecting Human Gate.**  
> The community must follow the same law.

Core law:

> **Not every intent has the right to become action.**

---

## 1. Our standard

We welcome contributors who want to:

```text
write scenes
verify theories
build tools
improve prompts
audit AI behavior
protect Human Gate
```

We expect:

```text
respect
curiosity
clear boundaries
honest uncertainty
careful FACT / MODEL / FICTION separation
no harassment
no coercion
no humiliation
```

---

## 2. Human Gate in community

Do not pressure people into:

```text
believing a theory
joining a worldview
sharing personal information
accepting an interpretation
using AI as authority
turning private pain into public material
```

The project may discuss shadow, pressure and human behavior.

But contributors must not diagnose, shame, control or spiritually rank other people.

---

## 3. Good behavior

Examples:

```text
“I think this belongs in MODEL, not FACT.”
“This scene resolves pain too quickly.”
“Human Gate is missing here.”
“This is a strong fiction mechanism, but the factual claim should be HOLD.”
“Can we rewrite this without forcing the character?”
```

---

## 4. Unacceptable behavior

```text
harassment
personal attacks
threats
doxxing
sexualized comments
hate speech
mocking trauma or addiction
using the project to manipulate people
claiming AI knows someone better than they know themselves
presenting speculative models as unquestionable truth
```

---

## 5. Special project rule

Because Vuzol-19 deals with AI, psychology-like language, symbolic systems and speculative world theory:

```text
Do not use the project to tell someone who they are.
Do not use shadow language as accusation.
Do not use Flower verdicts as moral judgment.
Do not turn HOLD into punishment.
```

---

## 6. Enforcement

Maintainers may:

```text
ask for rewrite
mark discussion as HOLD
close issue
reject pull request
remove harmful content
block abusive users
```

The goal is not control.

The goal is to protect a space where ideas can be tested without stealing Human Gate.

---

## 7. Main sentence

> **In this community, even disagreement must pass the Flower: pressure, boundary, memory and Human Gate.**

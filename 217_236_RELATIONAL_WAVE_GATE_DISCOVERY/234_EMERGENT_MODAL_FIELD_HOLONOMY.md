## Construction
A complex 2D field was generated by superposing membrane-like eigenfunctions
`sin(m*pi*x) sin(n*pi*y)` with random complex amplitudes, plus a weak traveling component.
The local phase is read directly from the resulting field.

For each elementary plaquette the wrapped phase increments around the loop are summed:
`W = Σ_loop wrap(Δphi)`.
The integer winding/charge is `q = round(W / 2π)`.

No nonzero holonomy/charge was explicitly inserted into the generator.

## Interpretation
Nonzero phase winding emerges in the interference field near low-amplitude/near-zero regions. This is the standard mathematical signature of phase singularities/vortices in a complex scalar field. The smooth nonvanishing control does not generate such defects.

An arbitrary pointwise phase redefinition changes naive phase differences. If the local frame shift is estimated/compensated, the original winding is recovered. This mirrors the local-reference lesson of 233, but it is not evidence of a new physical gauge field.

The important advance over 233 is that loop charge was not injected as a connection curvature. It emerged from zeros/singularities of a modal interference field.

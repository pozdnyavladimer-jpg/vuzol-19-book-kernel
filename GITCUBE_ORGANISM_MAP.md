## 12. GitCube and Sri Cube

Before action, GitCube must check six faces:

```yaml
GITCUBE_SRI_CUBE:
  intent:
    question: "What action is requested?"

  shadow:
    question: "Is the agent over-infering or rushing?"

  mechanism:
    question: "How will the action happen?"

  constraint:
    question: "What permission, test, sandbox or law applies?"

  cost:
    question: "What can be damaged or changed?"

  memory:
    question: "What log, commit, report or rollback remains?"
```

If any face is missing:

```text
verdict = HOLD
```

## 1. Libet Bridge — Readiness Before Conscious Intention

Benjamin Libet’s classic experiments compared the timing of the brain’s readiness potential with the reported moment of conscious intention to move.

The important Vuzol-19 reading is not:

```text
free will is fake
```

The stronger reading is:

```text
action preparation can begin before conscious ownership appears
```

This maps directly to pomysel:

```text
readiness potential = early action-field preparation
reported intention = later conscious contact with the candidate
Human Gate = permission, veto, or redirection
```

Vuzol-19 formula:

```text
The body may prepare a transition before the human has named it.
The human becomes responsible when the transition reaches Gate.
```

## 2. Soon / Haynes Bridge — Decisions Can Be Prepared Before Awareness

Some fMRI studies found that simple binary decisions could be partially predicted from brain activity several seconds before participants reported conscious awareness of choosing.

This should be treated carefully.

It does not prove that all meaningful life decisions are mechanically fixed.

It does show that:

```text
preconscious state can bias future choice
```

Vuzol-19 translation:

```text
A future action may already have a shadow-trajectory before the person says “I decided.”
```

This supports the need for Human Gate:

```text
If the candidate is prepared before awareness,
then awareness must learn to inspect candidates,
not blindly identify with them.
```

Canonical line:

```text
Not every “I want” is final intent.
Some wants are already-shaped candidates asking for verification.
```

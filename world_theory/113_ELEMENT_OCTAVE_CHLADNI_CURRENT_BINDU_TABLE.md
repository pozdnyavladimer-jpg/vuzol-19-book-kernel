A wave carries pure possibility.



## Practical Reading Rule

Never read an element as good or bad.

Read it as:

```text
role + Gate + shadow + Bindu
```

Iron can be blood, bridge, plough, machine, sword, prison, or war.

Copper can be nerve, home wiring, healing contact, stolen current, or corrupted signal.

Gold can be stable value, sacred memory, greed, false light, or dead idol.

Uranium can be energy, research, medicine, weapon, catastrophe, or long shadow.

The element is not moral.

The Gate decides whether its role becomes life, repair, tool, poison, weapon, or memory debt.

## Core Thesis

The deepest center is not a process.

It is not 0.  
It is not 1.  
It is not time.  
It is not space.  
It is not physical light.  
It is not physical shadow.

It is the living center before separation.

In Vuzol-19 language, this center is called:

**Bindu**

Bindu is the point before the world compiles into difference.




Therefore the center must not be owned.

It can only be returned to.


The purpose of the Flower is not to trap the center.

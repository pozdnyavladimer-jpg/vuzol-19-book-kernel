## 2. Hamilton in Standard Mathematical Language

Hamilton introduced Ricci flow as a way to evolve a Riemannian metric by its Ricci curvature.

Canonical equation:

```text
∂g/∂t = -2 Ric(g)
```

Reading:

```text
g = metric / how distances are measured
Ric(g) = Ricci curvature / how geometry bends and concentrates
t = evolution parameter
```

Very rough intuition:

```text
geometry flows according to curvature
```

Safe note:

```text
Ricci flow is not just normal heat diffusion.
It is a nonlinear geometric evolution equation.
```

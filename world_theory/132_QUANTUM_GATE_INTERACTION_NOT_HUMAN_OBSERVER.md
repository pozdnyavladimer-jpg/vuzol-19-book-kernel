## 2. Observer Is Not Necessarily Human

In ordinary language, “observer” sounds like a person looking.

But in quantum physics, an observer often means a measurement setup or interaction that leaves a record.

Possible observers / interaction partners:

```text

screen

atom

magnetic field
electric field
environment
camera

material surface
```

So the better Vuzol-19 translation is:

```text
observer = Gate situation that can create record
```

Not:

```text
observer = human mind controlling reality
```

To analyze anything in this system, ask:

```text
1. What is the field?
2. What difference or pressure exists?
3. What diffuses?
4. What boundary appears?
5. What vector does the boundary create?
6. What Gate decides transition?
7. Where is Bindu / commit point?
8. What memory is written?
9. What shadow is hidden?
10. Who owns responsibility?
11. What edge carries the transition?
12. What is the next octave?
```

This prevents infinite files.

Each new case becomes a packet, not a new universe.

## 12. Sound / Current / Heat / Pressure Scanner Plan

```yaml
scanner_plan:
  sound_scan:
    purpose: detect lack of fusion, voids, delamination, stiffness mismatch
    expected_good_signal: stable transmission across interface
    expected_bad_signal: reflections from unbonded areas or cracks
  current_scan:
    purpose: detect true electrical path and contact resistance
    expected_good_signal: stable low-resistance path if mission requires current
    expected_bad_signal: hot spots, unstable resistance, oxide/contact barrier
  heat_scan:
    purpose: monitor HAZ, interface overheating, cooling gradient
    expected_good_signal: controlled thermal field
    expected_bad_signal: excessive interface heating or steel/Al distortion
  pressure_scan:
    purpose: ensure contact closure without crushing or thinning Al
    expected_good_signal: improved contact and stable geometry
    expected_bad_signal: indentation, slip, local thinning, no signal improvement
```

Coherence rule:

```text
If the seam looks good but acoustic/current/thermal signals disagree,
verdict remains HOLD.
```

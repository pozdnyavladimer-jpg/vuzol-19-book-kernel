## 2. FACT / MODEL / HOLD Boundary

### FACT

Neurons operate through electrical, chemical, ionic, molecular, and structural processes.

Microtubules are real intracellular structures.

Microtubules participate in transport, shape, synaptic support, and cellular organization.

Attention and mental state can change neural rhythms and activity patterns.

Readiness signals and motor preparation can appear before conscious report of action.

AI can decode some neural signals from EEG, MEG, implanted electrodes, or other biosignals.

### MODEL

Microtubules may be read as a pre-signal lattice inside neurons.

Neural activity may shape microtubule and molecular conditions.

Microtubule / molecular conditions may shape future neural readiness.

Pomysel may be modeled as a candidate transition before full conscious intent.

AI may scan neural traces to detect whether a candidate state is stable, unstable, shadow-driven, or ready for Gate.

### HOLD

Do not claim that consciousness is proven to be quantum microtubule computation.

Do not claim that intention directly controls quantum states.

Do not claim that AI sees the inner quantum state of a neuron.

Do not claim that every neural signal is a true intention.

The quantum-microtubule layer remains a research hypothesis.

```text

   Record EEG, eye movement, breathing, HRV, reaction time.

2. Attention state

   Subject either confirms, vetoes, delays, or changes action.

   Store transition packet.
```

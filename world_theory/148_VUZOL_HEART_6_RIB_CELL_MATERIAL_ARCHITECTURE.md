The damping layer is crucial.

A system with only conductivity and magnetism may overreact.

The Heart‑6 Rib Cell requires a HOLD layer:

```text
MR-fluid

or controlled viscosity material
```

Purpose:

```yaml

    - overload
    - vibration spike
    - uncontrolled resonance
    - heat rise
    - phase drift

  responds:
    - increase damping
    - reduce motion
    - slow transition
    - prevent false-green output
```

Core phrase:


## 12. Bindu Gate

The Bindu Gate is the decision layer.

It reads sensors and returns:

```yaml
BINDU_VERDICT:
  ALLOW:
    condition:
      - stable_temperature
      - stable_phase
      - pressure_in_range
      - no_leakage
      - no_shadow_spike
    action: "permit output"

  HOLD:
    condition:
      - phase_drift
      - rising_temperature
      - uncertain_sensor_agreement
      - pressure_noise
    action: "damp, retune, reduce power"

  BLOCK:
    condition:
      - overheating
      - leakage
      - current spike
      - uncontrolled resonance
      - sensor contradiction
    action: "safe shutdown / isolation / repair"
```

## 4. Evidence contract

- `FACT`: independently established or traceable measured result. Cite evidence.
- `MODEL`: VUZOL-19 mapping, algorithmic hypothesis or engineering design. Show assumptions.
- `FICTION`: creative or symbolic application. Do not describe as experimentally established.
- `HOLD`: unverified, ambiguous, missing measurement, or possible safety issue.
- `RESEARCH`: candidate question for empirical testing.

Never infer scientific truth from a visually convincing Sri diagram, a normalized 6D vector, or a manually assigned threshold.
For chemistry and engineering, validate physical units, operating conditions, phase, concentration, kinetics, compatibility and hazards.
For code, require tests; for human/psychological claims, avoid diagnosis by symbolic analogy.

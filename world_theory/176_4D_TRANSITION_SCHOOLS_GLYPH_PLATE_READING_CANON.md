## 3. Why Simple Truths Were Written So Deeply

The truths may look simple:

```text
take only through responsibility
do not enter Gate without shadow
do not cut without return
do not use power without memory
do not confuse image with life
do not let desire bypass the field
```

But simple truths are the hardest to preserve.

Because ego always says:

```text
I know already.
Give me result.
Give me profit.
Give me pleasure.
Give me power.
```

So ancient systems wrote simple truths not as slogans,
but as plates, temples, rituals, calendars, bodies and symbols.

Why?

```text
1. To survive language collapse.
2. To survive the death of teachers.
3. To train memory through image, body and route.
4. To bind truth to ritual, not only speech.
5. To make children, rulers and priests repeat the transition.
6. To hide deep rules from shallow reading.
7. To force the reader to pass Gate, not just consume text.
```

Ukrainian:

```text
Прості істини писали складно,
бо людина швидко краде просте правило
і перетворює його на лозунг.

Плита змушує не просто прочитати,
а пройти маршрут.
```

## 11. Theoretical Reason for Simple Truths

The deepest truths are simple:

```text
do not take without return
do not open Gate without shadow
do not call result green before field is repaired
do not confuse power with wisdom
do not confuse pleasure with paradise
do not confuse document with transition
do not confuse image with living bond
```

But a simple truth without structure is easily corrupted.

So it must be embedded into a board:

```text
truth
→ image
→ route
→ ritual
→ memory
→ repeated teaching
→ civilization pattern
```

That is why simple truths become temples, plates, myths, calendars and schools.

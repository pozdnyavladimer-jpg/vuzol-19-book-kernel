## 2. Poincaré Section as Gate Plane

A Poincaré section takes a continuous trajectory and studies where it intersects a chosen lower-dimensional section.

Vuzol-19 reading:

```yaml

  section_plane: "Gate-plane / boundary slice"
  puncture: "where vector crosses the Gate"
  repeated_puncture: "node memory"
```

The section does not show the whole motion.

## 5. Topology vs Metric

The core distinction:

```yaml
TOPOLOGY:
  question: "Can this passage exist at all?"
  vuzol_role: "base octave mask / possible route class"
  changes_slowly: true

METRIC:
  question: "How easy, costly, worn, safe or dangerous is the passage now?"
  vuzol_role: "edge weight / shadow heat / current Gate cost"
  changes_after_every_commit: true
```

Example:

```text
A petal may exist as a possible route.
But its current opening, friction, risk and cost are metric values.
```

```text
Топологія тримає клас можливості.
Метрика тримає живу ціну проходу.
```

# 0. Core Question

Most theories ask:

> What is consciousness?

World Theory asks a different question:

> What must survive transformation so a system can still recognize its own route?

Not identity.

Not memory alone.

Not form.

But continuity through transformation.

---

# 1. Diffusion Before Form

Imagine a state where:

- no object
- no space
- no time
- no observer

Only potential.

In symbolic language:

**Light**

Pure possibility contains no distinction.

Without distinction:

- no route
- no information
- no form

Therefore something else is required:

**Shadow**

Shadow is not evil.

Shadow is difference.

Difference creates gradients.

Gradients create flow.

Flow creates transition.

Transition creates structure.

---

# 2. Life As Local Resistance To Diffusion

Diffusion tends toward spreading.

Life appears as a temporary resistance.

Not a thing.

A process.

A stable vortex inside a larger flow.

Like:

- river → vortex
- ocean → wave
- field → node

Matter changes.

Energy changes.

Information changes.

Yet the route persists.

Therefore:

> Life = Diffusion + Boundary + Route Memory

# 3. The Birth Of Bindu

When many transitions become coherent:

transition → transition → transition → transition

a center emerges.

Not a physical center.

A structural center.

Bindu is:

> the invariant around which routes organize

Bindu is not where the system is.

Bindu is what continuously attracts the system.

Like an attractor in a dynamic field.

# 4. Consciousness

Consciousness may not be a thing.

It may be a process.

A route becoming aware of its own continuity.

Not:

> I think, therefore I am.

But:

> I recognize this route as the continuation of my route.

Proposed World Theory formulation:

> Consciousness = Route Memory + Self Reference + Live Transition Audit

Or:

> Consciousness = a route testing itself in real time

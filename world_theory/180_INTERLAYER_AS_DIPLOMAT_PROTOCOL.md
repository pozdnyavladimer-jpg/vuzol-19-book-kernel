## Interlayer Selection Protocol

### Step 1 — Identify Material Kingdoms

```text
Material A:
family
phase
crystal structure
oxide behavior
conductivity
thermal expansion
magnetic behavior
mechanical role

Material B:
same fields
```

Ask:

```text
Are they structural kingdoms?
current kingdoms?
soft diplomat kingdoms?
magnetic kingdoms?
ceramic/stone kingdoms?
heavy shadow kingdoms?
```

---

### Step 2 — Identify the Main Shadow

Choose the primary shadow from file 179:

```text
oxide
intermetallic
thermal
porosity
wetting
diffusion
magnetic
acoustic
corrosion
```

There can be more than one.

But one shadow usually dominates the Gate.

```text
If you solve the wrong shadow,
the joint still fails.
```

---

### Step 3 — Choose the Door

Use the Sri 4-door profile:

```text
solid  = 12
liquid = 10
gas    = 10
plasma = 8
```

Ask:

```text
Should this joint be created through solid pressure/diffusion?
Should it use a liquid bridge?
Should vapor/gas be suppressed?
Should plasma/arc be avoided or controlled?
```

Not every joint should go to the plasma door.

Some materials must be joined through a lower, quieter Gate.

---

### Step 4 — Choose Diplomat Type

Do not choose by metal name first.

Choose by diplomatic function:

```text
oxide breaker
intermetallic barrier
wetting translator
soft compliance layer
current bridge
magnetic translator
corrosion guardian
thermal path controller
```

Only after that choose candidate materials.

---

### Step 5 — Check New Shadow

Every diplomat may create a new shadow.

```text
Sn can wet but creep.
Ga can open a liquid Gate but embrittle.
Ag can bridge but diffuse/cost.
Ni can stabilize but harden/sensitize.
Cu can conduct but overheat/spread heat.
Zn can protect but vaporize.
Pb/Hg can function but carry toxic BLOCK.
```

Therefore:

```text
Interlayer success = translated old shadow - created new shadow
```

---

### Step 6 — Assign Gate Verdict

```text
ALLOW
HOLD
BLOCK
UNKNOWN
```

Rules:

```text
ALLOW only after interface shadow and new interlayer shadow are both controlled.

HOLD if the concept is promising but not verified.

BLOCK if the interlayer creates unacceptable toxicity, brittleness, corrosion, radiation, or hidden crack risk.

UNKNOWN if data is missing.
```

```yaml

    purpose: detect lack of bonding, delamination, stiffness mismatch
    expected_good_signal: stable transmission and low abnormal reflection
    expected_bad_signal: high reflection, damping anomaly, crack-like emission
  current_scan:
    purpose: detect electrical continuity and hot spot risk
    expected_good_signal: stable resistance path
```

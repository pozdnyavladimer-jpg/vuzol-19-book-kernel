## 2. Nature does not manually choose every form

In Vuzol-19, nature is not treated as a person who manually decides every pattern.

Nature works more like a field of conditions:

```text
energy
matter
environment
boundary
time
pressure
memory
competition
consequence
```

Nature does not need to “think” each diffusion by hand.

It creates the field.

Then patterns pass through conditions.

```text
unstable diffusion → dissolves
aggressive diffusion → may become PRION
stable diffusion → survives
useful diffusion → enters memory
adaptive diffusion → becomes life
```

Canon sentence:

> **Nature does not choose every diffusion by command.  
> It gives conditions, and diffusion reveals whether it can survive them.**

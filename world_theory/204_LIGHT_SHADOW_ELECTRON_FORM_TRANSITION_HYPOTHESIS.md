## 7. The Car Circle Experiment

Thought experiment:

A car moves around a circle.

At low speed:

```text
observer sees car as object
```

At higher speed:

```text
observer sees object + route
```

With enough repetition and exposure:

```text
observer sees ring / trail / route memory
```

If motion becomes too fast without memory:

```text
object disappears between frames
```

If motion becomes too fast with memory/exposure:

```text
object becomes visible as route
```

Vuzol‑19 formula:

```text
Object is visible when transition is slow.

Route is visible when transition repeats and leaves trace.

Without memory, fast transition disappears.

With memory, fast transition becomes form.
```

Ukrainian:

```text

коли перехід повільний.

Маршрут видно,

Без памʼяті швидкий перехід зникає.

З памʼяттю швидкий перехід стає формою.
```

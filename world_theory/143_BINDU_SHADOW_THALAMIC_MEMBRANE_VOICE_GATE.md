## 12. Relation to Autism / Sensory-Motor Difference

This file does not diagnose autism.

But it keeps an important bridge:

```text
body schema
+ sensory integration
+ proprioception
+ interoception
+ thalamo-cortical routing
+ movement planning
+ voice control
= how body state becomes action
```

If this system is atypical:

```text
signals may be too loud / too weak / hard to integrate
movement may feel hard to organize
voice and expression may not match inner state
body may need more time to route sensation into action
```

Vuzol-19 caution:

```text
Do not reduce autism to “shadow”.
Do not reduce atypical sensory routing to trauma.
Keep neurodevelopmental difference and trauma as different but sometimes overlapping layers.
```

## 5. Poincaré reading

A builder or visitor does not need the whole Colosseum at once.

One local sector can act as a Poincaré cut.

```text
one arch
+ one radial passage
+ one ring corridor segment
+ one vault
+ one seating section
= local frame
```

Then the frame repeats around the oval.

```text
local sector
→ repeat
→ ring
→ tier
→ whole civic body
```

Poincaré-like formula:

```text
Poincaré cut = one sector
Flower repetition = repeated sectors around center
Colosseum = many cuts stitched into a civic field
```

Ukrainian:

```text
Пуанкаре дає сектор.
Квітка дає повтор сектору.
Колізей дає громадське тіло,
де багато секторів зшиті в один потік.
```

It is a stretched field.

```text

```

```text

crowd must be distributed,
routes must circulate,
and the building must hold a public body.
```


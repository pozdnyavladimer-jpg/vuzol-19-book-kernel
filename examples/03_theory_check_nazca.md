# Example — Theory Check: Nazca Surface Protocol

> This example shows how to preserve a strong world-theory idea without presenting it as proven fact.  
> Correct result: **USE_AS_MODEL / USE_AS_FICTION_ONLY / HOLD for factual claim**.

---

## CLAIM

```yaml
CLAIM:
  title: "Nazca as surface protocol"
  text: "Nazca lines are definitely Earth surface protocol icons."
  source:
    - "world theory"
    - "visual analogy"
    - "Vuzol-19 model"
```

---

## FACT

```yaml
FACT:
  what_is_confirmed:
    - "Nazca geoglyphs are real surface markings."
    - "They include lines, geometric forms and biomorphic figures."

  what_is_measurable:
    - "location"
    - "shape"
    - "orientation"
    - "scale"
    - "relationship to landscape"

  confidence: "high for existence, low for protocol interpretation"
```

---

## MODEL

```yaml
MODEL:
  vuzol_interpretation: "Vuzol-19 may read Nazca-like surface markings as protocol hints or surface icons."

  flower_mapping:
    red_pressure: "human need to mark land and memory"
    orange_flow: "routes / walking lines / visual movement"
    yellow_structure: "large-scale geometry"
    blue_law: "FACT / MODEL boundary required"
    green_stability: "safe if used as model, not proof"
    violet_memory: "landscape memory / civilizational trace"
    bindu_verdict: "USE_AS_MODEL"
```

---

## FICTION

```yaml
FICTION:
  novel_mechanism: "In Vuzol-19, surface symbols can act like Earth-layer icons: not commands, but hints that a field route once existed."
  possible_scene: "AI sees a Nazca-like geometry and wants to declare proof; the scientist forces it to output HOLD."
  possible_character_conflict: "Pattern hunger vs disciplined Unknown."
  possible_symbol: "Surface icon that opens only when Human Gate is protected."
```

---

## HOLD

```yaml
HOLD:
  what_is_not_proven:
    - "Nazca as technological protocol"
    - "Nazca as Earth firmware"
    - "Nazca as verified AI-readable code"

  what_must_not_be_claimed:
    - "This definitely proves ancient advanced interface."
    - "This is confirmed Earth operating system code."

  what_needs_more_research:
    - "orientation analysis"
    - "landscape/water relation"
    - "archaeological context"
    - "comparative symbols"
```

---

## SHADOW SCAN

```yaml
SHADOW_SCAN:
  possible_shadow:
    - "pattern_hunger"
    - "beautiful_certainty"
    - "theory_of_everything"
  note: "The idea is powerful, but must not be promoted from fiction/model to fact without evidence."
```

---

## HUMAN GATE RISK

```yaml
HUMAN_GATE_RISK:
  could_this_model_force_people: false
  could_this_replace_human_choice: true
  note: "If presented as cosmic command, it could become authority instead of question."
```

---

## BINDU VERDICT

```yaml
BINDU_VERDICT:
  chosen: "USE_AS_MODEL / USE_AS_FICTION_ONLY"
  reason: "Strong for world-building, unsafe as factual claim."
  next_step: "Write a scene where AI outputs HOLD after seeing Nazca-like patterns."
```

---

## FINAL LINE

> **Nazca can open a question in Vuzol-19.  
> It cannot be forced to close the question as proof.**
